# Credits

## The stand-in tape

The video in the live demo (`docs/media/demo-v1.low.mp4`) and in every picture in this repository is:

> **Bob Behnken discusses the SpaceX Demo-2 mission**
> NASA Johnson Space Center, 2020
> NASA ID `jsc2020m000131_Bob_Behnken_discusses_the_SpaceX_Demo-2_mission`, from [images.nasa.gov](https://images.nasa.gov/)

It is shown whole and uncut, re-encoded with this tool's own low-copy settings (480p, about 0.55 Mbps, a keyframe every second).

The transcript in `site/demo-transcript.json` is NASA's caption file for that video, with the short caption lines joined into sentences and changed from capitals to sentence case. The words are unchanged.

Source: NASA. NASA content is generally not subject to copyright in the United States. It is used here in a factual way, as an example tape. **NASA does not endorse this project**, and nothing here should be read as suggesting that it does. The NASA insignia visible on the flight suit is NASA's and is not part of this project's branding.

The MIT licence in this repository covers the code. It does not cover this footage or its transcript.

## The measurements

The jump and playback timings quoted in the README and on the project page were taken on a 63-minute interview tape, played in Chromium through a proxy throttled to 1 and then 2 megabits per second: five jumps and thirty seconds of playback for each copy at each speed.
